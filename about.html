Where we started
A simple frustration,
turned into a business.

Escape Tour and Travels started the way most good visa consultancies do: from watching too many good applications get rejected for avoidable reasons. Missing documents. Vague cover letters. Wrong visa category entirely. None of that is about luck; it's about knowing exactly what an embassy is actually looking for.

Eight years and over 2000 applications later, that's still the whole job: read the requirements more carefully than anyone else, tell clients honestly what their chances are, and file a complete, correct application the first time. That discipline is where the 98% approval rate actually comes from: not a trick, just not skipping the boring parts.
